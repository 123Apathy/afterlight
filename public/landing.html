Questions

What families ask.

How does my family add their photos?

With one WhatsApp link. You share it, they tap it, and they're in. There's nothing to download or install, and no accounts or passwords.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You start with just their name. No sign-up, no downloads, and your memorial is ready to share in seconds.

Who can see our photos?

Your memorial isn't listed anywhere public. It opens only through its private invite link, the one your family shares on WhatsApp.

What do we receive at the end?

A cinematic HD film for the service, with an original song written from your memories. The film and the gallery are yours to keep, forever.

When will the film be ready?

Your film is ready to watch and download 24 hours before the due date, and within 48 hours of everyone's choices being submitted or the deadline being reached, whichever comes first.
